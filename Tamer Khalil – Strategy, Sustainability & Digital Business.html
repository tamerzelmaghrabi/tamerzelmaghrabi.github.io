About me

I’m a Master’s student at TH Wildau, majoring in Sustainability and Marketing, and I live in Berlin. Before moving to Germany I worked in travel web development, finance and resort operations in Egypt.

It all started with digital marketing, where I learned how an online presence turns into real customers. At Sky Elgandoul Resort I moved into hospitality, first in accounting and then as assistant operations manager, and I saw up close how a business runs day to day.

I joined Octopus Travel when it operated from a single branch in Cairo, and I helped scale the business by launching and managing our second location in Hurghada. Alongside co-directing operations with my partners, I took full ownership of our digital infrastructure, building the website from scratch and setting up the digital processes behind it.

Today I study European Business Management and bring that hands-on experience into every course and project, while finding my place in the German academic and professional world.
